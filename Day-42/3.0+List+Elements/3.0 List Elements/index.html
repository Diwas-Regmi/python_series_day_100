Homemade Chocolate Chip Cookies

Ingredients

For the cookies:

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • ½ cup butter
  • ½ cup white sugar
  • ½ cup brown sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • ½ teaspoon baking soda
  • ¼ teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup chocolate chips

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F or 175°C.
  2. Melt the butter and allow it to cool slightly.
  3. Mix the melted butter with the white sugar and brown sugar until well combined.
  4. Add the egg and vanilla extract, then mix until smooth.
  5. Add the flour, baking soda, and salt to the mixture and stir until a soft dough forms.
  6. Fold the chocolate chips into the dough.
  7. Scoop small portions of the dough and place them onto a baking tray lined with parchment paper.
  8. Leave some space between each cookie because they will spread while baking.
  9. Bake the cookies for 10–12 minutes until the edges become lightly golden.
  10. Remove the cookies from the oven and let them cool for a few minutes before serving.
