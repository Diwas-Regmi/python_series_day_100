Cooking Tips

  • Homemade Chocolate Chip Cookies

  • Ingredients

    1. For the Cookies

      1. All-purpose flour

      2. Butter

      3. White sugar

      4. Brown sugar

      5. Egg

      6. Vanilla extract

      7. Baking soda

      8. Salt

      9. Chocolate chips

    2. For Baking

      1. Baking tray

      2. Parchment paper

  • Instructions

    1. Prepare the Oven

    2. Prepare the Dough

      • Melt the butter

        1. Let the butter cool
        2. Mix with the sugars

      • Add the egg and vanilla

      • Add flour, baking soda, and salt

    3. Bake the Cookies

      1. Place dough on baking tray

      2. Bake for 10–12 minutes

      3. Let the cookies cool
